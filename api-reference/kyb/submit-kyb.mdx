---
title: "Submit KYB"
api: "POST https://pay-api.holdstation.com/partners/kyb"
description: "Submit a business verification (KYB) record for your partner account."
---

Submit a business verification (KYB) record for your partner account. The record id is generated by Holdstation Pay and returned as `id` — do not send one.

A partner holds at most one KYB record. While a submission is **Pending** (`1`) or **Approved** (`2`), another `POST` fails with `409 Conflict`. Once a record is **Rejected** (`3`), `POST` again to replace it — the record keeps its original id, and the files attached to the rejected submission are deleted.

See the [Partner Business KYB guide](/guides/partner-business-kyb/overview) for the lifecycle, the [Field Reference](/guides/partner-business-kyb/field-reference) for every field, and [Submitting Documents](/guides/partner-business-kyb/submitting-documents) for file rules.

## Authentication

<ParamField header="Partner-App-Key" type="string" required>
  Your partner app key.
</ParamField>

<ParamField header="X-HSPay-Signature" type="string" required>
  Base64-encoded Ed25519 signature of the sign data.
</ParamField>

<ParamField header="X-HSPay-Timestamp" type="string" required>
  Unix timestamp (seconds) of the request. Must be within 60 seconds of server time.
</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<Note>
  Every field below is mandatory, including the `persons` and `documents` arrays. The only optional fields on a submission are `date_of_issue` and `date_of_expiry` inside a document object.
</Note>

<ParamField body="subject_type" type="integer" required>
  `1` = the partner itself, `2` = a merchant under the partner.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="legal_name" type="string" required>
  Registered company name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="registration_number" type="string" required>
  Business registration number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="incorporation_country" type="string" required>
  ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="incorporation_date" type="string" required>
  Incorporation date in ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="address" type="string" required>
  Registered business address.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="email" type="string" required>
  Business email.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="phone" type="string" required>
  Business phone number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="website" type="string" required>
  Business website.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="nature_of_business" type="string" required>
  Description of the business activity.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="geographic_scope" type="string[]" required>
  ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes the business operates in.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="persons" type="object[]" required>
  Legal representatives and directors. Each entry requires `role` (`1` = Legal representative, `2` = Director), `name`, `date_of_birth`, `email`, `phone`, `address`, `id_number`, `id_type` (`1` = Citizen ID, `2` = Passport, `3` = License), `sex` (`1` = Male, `2` = Female, `3` = Other), `nationality`, `date_of_issue`, `date_of_expiry`, and `documents` of type `10` or `11` only.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="documents" type="object[]" required>
  Company documents, types `1`–`9` only. Each entry requires `type`, `document_number`, and `files` (max 10 per document). `date_of_issue` and `date_of_expiry` are optional — omit them for documents with no issue or expiry date.
</ParamField>

## Document Types

| Value | Type | Belongs in |
|---|---|---|
| `1` | Incorporation paper | `documents[]` |
| `2` | Business registration extract | `documents[]` |
| `3` | Nature and scope declaration | `documents[]` |
| `4` | Import/export licence | `documents[]` |
| `5` | Product images | `documents[]` |
| `9` | Other | `documents[]` |
| `10` | Passport | `persons[].documents[]` |
| `11` | Citizen ID | `persons[].documents[]` |

## File Rules

Files are sent inline, base64-encoded in `data`. Accepted `content_type` values are `application/pdf`, `image/jpeg`, `image/png`, and `image/heic`; the declared type must match the actual bytes, and encrypted PDFs are rejected. Each file is capped at **10 MB** decoded, and the whole request body at **40 MB**. Files are returned as metadata only — `file_ref`, `filename`, `content_type`, `size` — never as bytes.
